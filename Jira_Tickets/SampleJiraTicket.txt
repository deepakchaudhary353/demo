Summary: PrinterService crashes during high-volume print jobs
Environment: Production
Version: v3.2.1
Priority: Critical
Status: In Progress
Assignee: Alice
Reporter: QA-Team
Created: 2023-11-15
Updated: 2023-11-18

Description:
During peak hours, PrinterService crashes intermittently when processing large batches of print jobs (>500 pages). The issue occurs after approximately 20 minutes of continuous operation. Users report that the printer stops responding and jobs remain in the queue indefinitely.

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Log in to PrinterService dashboard.
2. Submit a batch print job with 500+ pages.
3. Observe system behavior after 15-20 minutes.

Observed Behavior:
- PrinterService process terminates unexpectedly.
- No error message displayed to the user.
- Print queue shows jobs as 'In Progress' indefinitely.

Expected Behavior:
- PrinterService should handle high-volume jobs without crashing.
- Jobs should complete successfully or fail gracefully with an error message.

Error Pattern:
System.OutOfMemoryException: Insufficient memory to continue execution of the program.
